PL-CD

Casing drift

The PL-CD Casing Drift is designed to drift the production casing before running downhole equipment and to verify the internal diameter of the casing string by simulating the length and maximum outside diameter of the equipment to be run.

About the tool

Aluminium drift bushings are used as gauging elements. The bushings feature longitudinal grooves that allow fluid circulation during the run. Compared with steel bushings, aluminium bushings provide easier retrieval of the drift if it becomes stuck in the casing and provide a clear indication of casing damage or changes in the internal diameter.

Design advantages

  • Capability to simulate the dimensions of downhole equipment to be run;
  • Allows fluid circulation during the drift run;
  • Enables identification of damaged or restricted casing intervals;
  • Allows casing drift verification by axial movement of the work string.
