Casing centralizer-turbolizer

The Centralizer-Turbulizer is designed for installation on casing strings to facilitate smooth casing running and maintain the casing in a centralized position within the wellbore, helping to achieve a uniform cement sheath around the casing.

Category
Casing equipment
Request a selection

About the tool

The turbulizer promotes circumferential flow around the casing and increases turbulence in the annulus. This significantly improves the efficiency of drilling-fluid displacement by the cement slurry within the turbulizer operating zone, particularly in enlarged or cavernous sections of the wellbore and when the casing string is positioned eccentrically.

The Centralizer-Turbulizer is suitable for use in vertical, deviated, and horizontal oil and gas wells.
