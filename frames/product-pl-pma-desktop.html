PL-PMA

Polish mill assembly

The PL-PMA is designed to clean and prepare the polished bore receptacle (PBR) for reliable installation of the tieback packer or tieback seal assembly. The assembly removes cement, mud solids, scale and other debris from the PBR bore, providing a clean and smooth sealing surface for subsequent tieback operations.

FEATURES AND BENEFITS

  • Polishes and conditions the PBR sealing surface
  • Self-centralizing for reliable tool alignment
  • Available in various PBR sizes and configurations
  • Provides a clean, smooth sealing surface
  • Helps prevent damage to tieback seals
  • Improves tieback sealing reliability
  • Reduces the risk of unsuccessful installation
  • Enables efficient PBR preparation before re-entry
