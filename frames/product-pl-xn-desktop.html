About the tool

The landing nipples feature an internal polished sealing bore and a locking profile for installing, positioning, and securing downhole devices. The sealing bores and locking profiles are designed to be compatible with various tool and locking systems. The nipples are available in full-bore and reduced-bore configurations and provide a reliable positive stop for downhole flow-control devices.

The landing nipples are sized to match the corresponding tubing sizes. Sealing bore diameters are available in different configurations to accommodate various tubing wall thicknesses and connection systems.

The PL-XN Landing Nipple is designed as the bottom element of the tubing string. It incorporates a No-Go shoulder that prevents downhole tools and devices from passing through the nipple.

The most common Otis-type landing nipple profiles are available.

Design advantages

  • Polished internal sealing bore for reliable sealing;
  • High-strength design.
