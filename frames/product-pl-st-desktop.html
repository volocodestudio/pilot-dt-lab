PL-ST

Stinger

The Stinger is designed for cementing large-diameter casing strings through the drill pipe. It reduces the volume of fluid required for displacement, thereby shortening cementing time and improving operational safety.

About the tool

A separate drill pipe plug is supplied with the Stinger PL-ST.

Design advantages

  • Provides effective control of the cementing process.
  • Enables disconnection using a left-hand thread.
  • Allows installation of either a single-valve or double-valve configuration.
