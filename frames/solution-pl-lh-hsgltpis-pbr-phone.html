PL-LH/HSGLTPIS/PBR

Hydraulic single-grip liner hanger with liner top packer & inner string

Being verified

The hydraulic single-grip liner hanger with liner top packer & inner string is designed to provide precise and reliable positioning of the tailpipe within the casing without the need for cementing, ensuring effective wellbore preparation prior to subsequent operations.

About the system

The system allows controlled circulation of wash fluid through the sealing coupling into which the stinger is inserted, enabling efficient removal of cuttings from the wellbore.

The tool controls wash-fluid flow and minimizes pressure losses while allowing safe disconnection of the tailpipe after the washing operation is complete. The system can be reciprocated and rotated during running in hole.

The liner hanger allows connection of a tieback extension string and, if required, installation of a mechanical liner top packer.

Design advantages

  • Enables running and setting operations in complex geological and drilling conditions;
  • Wide operating pressure range;
  • High reliability and ease of operation;
  • Effectively cleans the wellbore during the running liner filter.
