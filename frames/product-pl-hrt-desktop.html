PL-HRT

Hydraulic disconnector

The hydraulic disconnector is designed for running and setting of a casing liner. The disconnector design allows the liner string to be rotated and moved axially within an acceptable range.

About the tool

The PL-HRT disconnector is used in both vertical and directional wells. It is used with various types of completion and running equipment, primarily in combination with liner hangers, and is suitable for liner strings with a low casing weight. Disconnection of the drill pipe from the casing liner is performed by applying a predetermined activation pressure.

A stop collar and a drill pipe plug or ball are supplied with the disconnector.

Design advantages

  • Running and setting of liners in difficult geological and technological conditions;
  • Rotation of the liner string during running;
  • Reliability and ease of use.
