Latch seal for permanent packer (Permapack)

The Latch Seal is designed to provide a hermetic connection between the tubing string and a pre-installed permanent packer.

Category
Completion equipment
Request a selection

About the tool

The Latch Seal design provides a reliable and pressure-tight connection to the packer by means of a threaded collet and sealing nipple. An additional positive shoulder is incorporated into the design to provide a positive stop for the latch in the upper section of the packer.

Design advantages

  • Simple and reliable connection to the permanent packer;
  • Easy disconnection of the tubing string from the packer;
  • High sealing performance of the sealing elements.
