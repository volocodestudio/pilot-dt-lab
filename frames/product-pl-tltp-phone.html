PL-TLTP

Tieback liner top packer

The tieback liner top packer is designed to provide a reliable, high-pressure seal between the liner and casing after liner installation and cementing. The packer is run after the liner PBR seal bore has been cleaned out and is mechanically weight-set to seal the annular space between the liner and casing.

Category
Liner hangers
Request a selectionSize table

About the tool

The packer incorporates a seal assembly designed to provide a pressure-tight connection with the PBR of the liner hanger, ensuring reliable sealing during subsequent tieback and completion operations. The packer element is available in various elastomer compounds to accommodate different wellbore conditions and temperature requirements.

The Tieback Liner Top Packer can also be used where the head of a previously installed liner hanger system with PBR does not provide the required pressure integrity. The packer is run onto the head of the previously installed liner and mechanically set to establish an additional pressure-tight sealing barrier between the liner and casing for subsequent well operations.

FEATURES AND BENEFITS

  • High differential pressure capability from above and below.
  • Mechanical running lock prevents premature setting during deployment.
  • Full-bore design provides unrestricted access for subsequent tool runs.
  • Hardened hold-down slips provide reliable axial anchoring.
  • Internal body lock ring positively retains the applied setting force.
  • Integral PBR provides a sealing interface for subsequent tieback operations.
  • Can be used to establish an additional pressure barrier when the existing liner hanger PBR does not provide the required sealing integrity.
  • High-pressure, high-temperature (HP/HT) sealing elements.
