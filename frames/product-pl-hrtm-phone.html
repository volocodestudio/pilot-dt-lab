PL-HRTM

Mechanical disconnector

The PL-HRTM mechanical disconnector is designed for running and cementing of a casing liner. During liner cementing, it maintains the liner in a straight position during the wait-on-cement period, thereby preventing the liner weight from being transferred to the cement sheath.

About the tool

The PL-HRTM disconnector can be equipped with a keying mechanism that prevents premature disconnection from the running casing string and allows the liner to be rotated during running, particularly in difficult well conditions.

The PL-HRTM disconnector is used in both vertical and directional wells and can be assembled with various types of technological equipment. The flushing holes are opened and the disconnection is performed by rotating the running tool to the right by the required number of turns.

A stop collar and a drill pipe plug or ball are supplied with the disconnector.

Design advantages

  • Running and setting of liners in difficult geological conditions;
  • Ensures control over the cementing process and enables cementing of the liner under tension;
  • Possibility of running and setting casing strings without using a liner hanger;
  • Disconnection using a left-hand thread;
  • Possibility of maintaining liner sections in a tensioned condition;
  • Cementing of liner sections under tension;
  • Backup option for opening the flushing holes;
  • Design features that allow the liner section to be rotated during running.
