About the system

During liner anchoring, the hanger maintains the liner in a stable and properly aligned position while the cement slurry sets, thereby preventing excessive load transfer to the cement sheath and ensuring effective bonding between the cement sheath and the casing. The packer provides reliable sealing of the annular space, preventing the migration of formation fluids during drilling operations.

Design advantages

  • Enables liner running and anchoring in wells with difficult geological and operational conditions;
  • Suitable for suspending liners with significant weight;
  • Reliable fixation of the anchor assembly during installation;
  • Reliable fixation of the gripping elements against both upward and downward axial movement;
  • Wide operating pressure range;
  • High mechanical strength;
  • High reliability and ease of operation.
