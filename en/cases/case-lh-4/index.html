Implementation of the PL-LH/PHDG Liner Hanger System with Polished Bore Receptacle (PBR)

Ukrgasvydobuvannya · Ukraine · Onshore

Conditions

Client
Ukrgasvydobuvannya
Country
Ukraine
Setting
Onshore
Well type
Gas
Well depth
16,946 ft (5,165 m)
Liner casing
4 1/2″ | 16.6 ppf | P-110
Liner length
3,494 ft (1065 m)
Open Hole
5 1/2″
Well inclination
24 deg
Packer Test Pressure
5,076 psi (350 bar)

Objectives

  • Operational Deployment: Safely run and actuate the liner assembly to the target depth under controlled hydraulic parameters.
  • Interface Integrity: Provide a high-precision PBR surface for subsequent completion operations.
  • Hydraulic Anchoring Performance: Ensure the liner hanger is reliably hydraulically set in the intermediate casing to withstand axial and hydraulic loads.
  • Zonal Isolation: Achieve effective annular sealing through optimized cement placement and precise packer activation.
  • Operational Efficiency: Minimize Non-Productive Time (NPT) and streamline the installation process.

Results

  • Successful Deployment: The liner reached the target depth (TD) using the PL-LH/PHDG system with PBR without operational interruptions.
  • Continuous Circulation: The actuation protection system maintained high-pressure circulation and cementing, preventing premature engagement of the hanger or packer.
  • Reliable Hydraulic Setting: The liner hanger was securely hydraulically anchored, and the running tool was released as planned, leaving the PBR surface clean and ready for completion.
  • Effective Isolation: Cementing and packer activation were executed precisely, achieving full zonal isolation and liner integrity within the project schedule.

Value to client

  • Completion Versatility: The high-precision PBR provides a reliable tie-back interface, enabling flexible future interventions and ensuring long-term connection integrity.
  • Wellbore Integrity: Superior anchoring and sealing maintain stable production and pressure containment throughout the well’s lifecycle.

Tools and equipment

  • Liner hanger (hydraulic)
  • Liner top packer (mechanical)
  • Disconnector (hydraulic)
  • Polished Bore Receptacle (PBR): 4.5-meter (15 ft) length

Products and systems

  • PL-LH/HSGHydraulic single-grip liner hanger
