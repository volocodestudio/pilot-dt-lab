Liner installation with the PL-LH/PHDG liner hanger system

Ukrgasvydobuvannya · Ukraine · Onshore

Conditions

Client
Ukrgasvydobuvannya
Country
Ukraine
Setting
Onshore
Well type
Gas
Liner casing
5″, 24.1 ppf, P-110
Liner length
492 ft (150 m)
Well depth
18,488 ft (5,635 m)
Open Hole
6″
Well inclination
22 deg
Packer Test Pressure
5,076 psi (350 bar)

Objectives

  • Secure Hanger Placement: Ensure the liner hanger is hydraulically set in the casing string to withstand both axial and hydraulic loads.
  • Effective Zonal Isolation: Activate the liner top packer to establish a reliable hydraulic seal and prevent fluid migration in the annulus.
  • Optimized Circulation: Maintain unobstructed annular flow to support high-rate circulation and efficient cement displacement.
  • Verified Tool Release: Confirm controlled and successful disengagement of the running tool following cementing operations.
  • Maximized Efficiency: Complete installation in a single trip to reduce operational downtime and eliminate non-productive time (NPT).

Results

  • Smooth Deployment: The liner and hanger assembly were successfully conveyed to the target depth without operational interruptions.
  • Accurate Positioning: Depth and alignment were verified prior to activating the hanger and packer system.
  • Reliable Hydraulic Anchoring: The liner hanger was securely set hydraulically, and the running tool was released as planned.
  • Effective Isolation: Cementing and packer installation ensured full zonal isolation and maintained liner structural integrity.
  • Efficient Execution: The operation was completed in a single continuous run without delays or technical issues.

Value to client

  • Enhanced Well Integrity: Proper hydraulic hanger setting and packer activation established a dependable hydraulic barrier and structural support.
  • Seamless Operations: Reliable isolation allowed uninterrupted progression to subsequent drilling and completion phases.
