Sidetrack operation with the PL-WSMSA “ANTHEUS” whipstock system

DOSCO / ZION oil & gas · Israel · Onshore

Conditions

Client
DOSCO / ZION oil & gas
Country
Israel
Setting
Onshore
Well type
Exploration well
Casing
9 5/8″ | 47 ppf | P-110
Setting depth
8848 ft (2,697 m)
Well inclination
10°
Ramp angle
2.5°

Objectives

  • Initiate a new wellbore from the cased hole (cased-hole sidetrack) by milling a casing window.
  • Mill a full-gauge casing window and establish a new wellbore in a single trip.
  • Maintain effective directional control throughout sidetracking operations.
  • Minimize nonproductive time (NPT) and optimize overall operational efficiency.

Results

  • The PL-WSMSA Whipstock System and Milling BHA were successfully run to the planned setting depth in the MJ-2 well.
  • The whipstock was accurately oriented using a mud-pulse MWD telemetry system.
  • The mechanical anchor was set, and the milling BHA was released as planned.
  • Milling operations were, achieving a full-gauge casing exit and successfully drilling an 11.5-ft (3.5-m) rathole for sidetrack initiation.

Value to client

  • The full-gauge casing exit enabled efficient sidetrack drilling with a positive displacement motor (PDM), ensuring uninterrupted drilling operations.
  • The operation was completed in a single trip, reducing rig time and improving overall operational efficiency.
  • Pilot DT’s rapid mobilization and technical support enabled successful execution of the sidetrack operation, reducing rig time and improving overall project efficiency.

Products and systems

  • PL-WSMSAANTEUS™ whipstock system with mechanical anchor
