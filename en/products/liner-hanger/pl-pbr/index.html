PL-PBR

Polished bore receptacle

The Polished Bore Receptacle (PBR) is a precision-machined, honed tubular component designed to provide a smooth and reliable sealing surface for sealing elements. Tight manufacturing tolerances and a high-quality honed internal bore ensure consistent sealing performance.

Category
Liner hangers
Request a selectionSize table

FEATURES AND BENEFITS

  • Designed to accommodate bottom-set tieback packers and tieback seal stems.
  • Precision-honed bore provides a reliable sealing surface for tieback seal assemblies.
  • Tight manufacturing tolerances ensure consistent sealing performance.
  • Lead-in chamber facilitates smooth entry and running of tieback and completion tools.
  • Available in various lengths, materials and configurations to meet specific well design requirements.

About the tool

The upper end incorporates a specially machined lead-in chamber to facilitate smooth and reliable entry of drilling and completion tools. The lower end of the PBR can be configured for connection to a liner packer, setting sleeve, or other compatible liner hanger components.
