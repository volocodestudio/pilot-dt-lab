PL-PHO

Openhole hydraulic packer

The openhole hydraulic packer is designed to be set against the bottom of the well and to seal the bottomhole annular space. It is installed as part of the technological equipment assembly of a casing liner string.

Design advantages

  • Running and setting of liners in difficult geological and technological conditions;
  • Possibility of installation in an open hole or inside a casing string;
  • Wide range of operating pressure conditions;
  • Reliability and ease of use;
  • Packer element retention system.

About the tool

The packer is hydraulically activated by applying pressure to a predetermined setting pressure. An internal locking system positively retains the packer in the set position after the applied axial load is released, maintaining the required sealing force during subsequent operations.
