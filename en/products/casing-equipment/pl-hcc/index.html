PL-HCC

Hydraulic cementing collar

The hydraulic cementing collar is designed for running a casing liner as a single section, followed by collar cementing, as well as for two-stage cementing of the lower or upper casing section. It is primarily used for running and cementing liners with a filter section.

Design advantages

  • running and cementing wells under difficult geological and technological conditions;
  • redundant option for opening the flushing ports;
  • locking mechanism for the closing sleeve;
  • adjustable opening pressure of the flushing ports;
  • wide operating pressure range;
  • anti-rotation and secure fixation of the plugs;
  • PL-HCC coupling components designed to be drilled out with a PDC bit;
  • reliable and easy operation.

About the tool

PL-HCC cementing sleeves are used in combination with other process equipment, depending on the specific technological conditions of the well.

Equipment set includes

  • stage cementing collar;
  • stop collar;
  • bottom cementing plug;
  • top cementing plug.
