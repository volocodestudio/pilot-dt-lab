Hydraulic kit

The hydraulic kit is designed to adapt the window mill for use with a hydraulic whipstock configuration and provide a reliable pressure transmission path for activation of the hydraulic anchor. The kit enables the same window mill to be used with hydraulic whipstock systems without requiring a dedicated hydraulic mill configuration.

Category
Sidetracking
Request a selection

Design advantages

  • Adaptation of the window mill for hydraulic whipstock configurations
  • Reliable hydraulic pressure transmission to the hydraulic anchor
  • Sealed hydraulic interface within the window mill
  • Interchangeable nozzle inserts for activation pressure adjustment
  • Common window mill platform for mechanical and hydraulic configurations
  • Flexible configuration according to hydraulic anchor requirements
  • Simplified integration of the hydraulic system into the milling assembly

THE HYDRAULIC KIT INCLUDES

  • window mill adapter;
  • hydraulic line;
  • sealing sleeve with sealing rings;
  • range of interchangeable nozzle inserts.
  • The window mill adapter provides the connection between the milling assembly and the hydraulic line, while the hydraulic line transfers hydraulic pressure from the surface through the milling assembly to the hydraulic anchor.
  • The sealing sleeve is installed inside the window mill and provides a pressure-tight interface with the mill body. Sealing rings installed on the sleeve maintain hydraulic isolation and prevent pressure leakage during hydraulic anchor activation.
  • A range of interchangeable nozzle inserts with different internal diameters is provided to control the hydraulic flow and adjust the pressure required for activation of the hydraulic anchor. Selection of the appropriate nozzle insert allows the activation pressure to be matched to the requirements of the selected hydraulic anchor configuration.
  • The hydraulic kit provides a flexible solution for adapting the window mill to different hydraulic whipstock configurations while maintaining a common milling tool platform.
