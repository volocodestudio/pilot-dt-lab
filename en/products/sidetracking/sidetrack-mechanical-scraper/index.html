Whipstock scraper

The whipstock scraper is designed to prepare and clean the casing ID in the whipstock setting area before anchoring. The scraper removes scale, cement residue, debris and other deposits from the casing wall, helping to provide a clean and suitable surface for reliable whipstock setting.

Category
Sidetracking
Request a selection

Design advantages

  • For casing sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 13 3/8 in., with various casing wall thicknesses.
  • Effective removal of scale, cement residue and wellbore debris
  • Rapid integration into the whipstock deployment assembly
  • Reduced need for a dedicated casing-cleaning trip

About the tool

The scraper is designed for controlled contact with the casing wall and effective cleaning of the required setting interval. Its configuration supports wellbore preparation without requiring a separate dedicated scraper assembly where the selected whipstock configuration permits integrated deployment.

The whipstock scraper can be quickly integrated into the whipstock system and run as part of the selected deployment configuration, simplifying wellbore preparation and reducing additional rig operations.
