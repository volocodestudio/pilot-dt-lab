PL-DEP

Depth ejector jet pump

The PL-DEP depth ejector jet pump is designed for use in oil, gas, and gas-condensate fields as a downhole artificial lift system for producing formation fluids using the gas-lift jet method.

Category
Completion equipment
Request a selection

Design advantages

  • Suitable for deep wells and wells with low reservoir pressure;
  • Effective for producing high-viscosity fluids and fluids with a high liquid content;
  • Suitable for wells producing mechanical impurities from the formation;
  • Applicable in wells affected by deposits of resins, salts, and paraffin;
  • Available in stationary and insert-type configurations.

About the tool

The jet pump operates on the ejector principle, converting the energy of the power fluid into a high-velocity jet that entrains formation fluids and lifts the resulting mixture to the surface.

Stationary or insert-type jet pump assemblies can be used, depending on the well completion and operating conditions.

The PL-DEP is suitable for wells operating under difficult geological and technological conditions,

DEPTH EJECTOR JET PUMP INCLUDES

  • Great well depth;
  • Low reservoir pressure in productive formations;
  • High liquid content in produced fluids, particularly in oil wells;
  • High viscosity of produced fluids, including water-oil emulsions and crude oil;
  • Production of formation solids and mechanical impurities;
  • Deposits of resins, salts, and paraffin in the production system.
