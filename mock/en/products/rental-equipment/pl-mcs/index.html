PL-MCS

Mechanical casing scraper

The PL-MCS mechanical casing scraper is designed to clean the internal surface of casing strings from clay deposits, cement residue, paraffin, salt deposits, and other accumulated contaminants, including solid iron oxide deposits that may contribute to casing corrosion.

Design advantages

  • Provides effective coverage of the entire internal diameter of the casing string;
  • Allows efficient circulation and removal of scraped debris;
  • Capable of passing through sections with reduced casing internal diameter and through deviated or curved wellbore intervals.

About the tool

Cleaning and preparation of specific casing intervals are important operations when running tools for drilling, completion, and workover activities. The casing internal surface can be cleaned along the entire length of the casing string or within specified intervals by axially moving the scraper downward, with continuous or intermittent circulation of the flushing fluid to transport the removed debris to the surface.
