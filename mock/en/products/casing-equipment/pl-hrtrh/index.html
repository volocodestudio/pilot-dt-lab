PL-HRTRH

Hydraulic disconnector

The PL-HRTRH hydraulic disconnector is designed for running and setting of a casing liner. The disconnector design allows the liner string to be rotated and moved axially within an acceptable range.

Design advantages

  • Running and setting in difficult geological and technological conditions;
  • Availability of a backup disconnection method;
  • Anti-rotational design of the closing plug and reliable fixation;
  • Reliability and ease of use.

About the tool

The PL-HRTRH disconnector is used in both vertical and directional wells. It is used with various types of technological equipment, primarily in combination with liner hangers and stop assemblies, and is suitable for liner strings with a low casing weight.

Disconnection of the running tool after completion of all technological operations is performed by applying a predetermined activation pressure. If the required seal is not achieved, the left-hand crossover is disengaged by rotating the drill string to the right.

A landing collar and a drill wiper plug or ball are supplied with the disconnector.
