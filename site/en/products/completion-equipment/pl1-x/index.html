PL1-X

Packer

The packer PL1-X is designed for the hermetic separation of two zones of the wellbore and isolation of the work string during the operation of oil, gas, and gas-condensate wells. It can be used in combination with other downhole equipment for various well stimulation and formation testing operations.

About the tool

The dual-grip design of the production packer allows it to be set in either tension or compression on the work string and enables the packer to withstand differential pressure from above or below.

The packer is designed to be retrievable, allowing pressure to be equalized above and below the packer and enabling easy release and retrieval of the upper slips.

The J-slot mechanism provides simple setting and retrieval. The packer is set by rotating the work string 1/4 turn to the right and retrieved by rotating the work string 1/4 turn to the right.

Design advantages

  • Withstands high differential pressures from above or below the packer;
  • Can be set in either tension or compression;
  • Field-proven release mechanism;
  • Robust and reliable design.
