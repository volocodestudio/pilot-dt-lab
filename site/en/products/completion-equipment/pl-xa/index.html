PL-XA

Sliding sleeve (SSD)

The PL-XA sliding sleeve is designed to establish communication between the tubing and annulus by moving the internal sleeve. The sleeve can be opened or closed using standard wireline or coiled tubing tools.

About the tool

Ports in the internal sleeve provide pressure equalization between the tubing and annulus when the sleeve is in the open position.

The PL-XA Sliding Sleeve is opened by moving the internal sleeve upward using an appropriate shifting tool. The sliding sleeve can be used to selectively direct flow between the tubing and annulus in multi-zone completion configurations.

Other applications include well killing, acid stimulation, hydraulic fracturing and pressure equalization between isolated formations and the tubing string.

Design advantages

  • Compact, simple, and field-proven design offering cost-effective and reliable operation with a long service life;
  • Manufactured from 4140 alloy steel with Viton® elastomers as standard;
  • Compatible with selective PL-X profile nipples for running and operating tools using wireline techniques.
