PL-LH/MSG

Mechanical single-grip liner hanger

The mechanical single-grip liner hanger is designed to suspend a liner casing string inside a previously run casing string, either after cementing or without cementing the well.

About the tool

During liner cementing, the hanger maintains the liner in a stable and properly aligned position while the cement slurry sets, thereby preventing excessive load transfer to the cement sheath.

The liner hanger can also be used for partial unloading of the tubing string.

The liner hanger is suitable for both vertical and directional wells and is compatible with various types of completion and running equipment.

The liner hanger is activated by applying tension to the work string over a length of 1–2 m, followed by rotating it 1–2 turns to the right and then setting down the required amount of weight.

Design advantages

  • Enables liner running and cementing in wells with difficult geological and operational conditions;
  • Allows cementing to be performed at different well intervals;
  • Reliable fixation of the gripping elements against downward axial movement;
  • Wide operating pressure range;
  • Easy deactivation;
  • High reliability and ease of operation.
