Design advantages

  • For casing sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 13 3/8 in., with various casing wall thicknesses.
  • For hole sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 12 1/4 in. (open-hole anchor)
  • Four anchoring configurations for different wellbore and sidetracking conditions
  • Direct integration with the whipstock concave
  • Mechanical, J-slot or hydraulic setting, depending on operational requirements
  • Reliable whipstock positioning throughout the milling sequence
  • Multiple slips for better rotational stability in the hydraulic configuration
  • MWD-based whipstock orientation with hydraulic and suspension anchors
  • No cement plug or bridge plug needed with hydraulic and suspension anchors
  • Setting at any required depth with the suspension anchor
  • Optional packer integration where additional isolation is required
  • Open-hole configuration for sidetracking in uncased wellbores
  • Retrievable cased-hole configurations for recovery of the whipstock assembly

ANCHORS

The anchoring system comes in four configurations for different sidetracking and wellbore conditions: mechanical anchor, hydraulic anchor, suspension anchor and open-hole mechanical anchor. Each anchor is mechanically integrated with the Whipstock Concave. This keeps the anchoring assembly securely connected to the whipstock during running, setting and milling, and holds the whipstock firmly in place throughout the sidetracking operation.

MECHANICAL ANCHOR

The mechanical anchor fixes the whipstock in cased-hole applications. It is set by applying the required axial load through the running string. The slip then engages the casing and prevents axial and rotational movement of the whipstock during milling. The anchor is retrievable, so the whipstock assembly can be recovered after the sidetracking operation is complete.

HYDRAULIC ANCHOR

The hydraulic anchor fixes the whipstock in cased-hole applications by hydraulic activation. Pressure is applied through the drill string and passes through the milling assembly and the external hydraulic line to the anchor. The pressure engages multiple slips with the casing, which prevents axial and rotational movement and keeps the whipstock stable during milling. No cement plug or bridge plug is needed as a base. The whipstock can be oriented with an MWD system before setting, and circulation is maintained below the setting pressure. An optional packer can be added where additional wellbore isolation is required.

SUSPENSION ANCHOR

The suspension anchor fixes the whipstock in cased-hole applications at any required depth, without a bridge plug or cement plug. The whipstock can be oriented with an MWD system before setting. The anchor is set mechanically by a specially machined indexing slot, which is operated by picking up and setting down the running string. Once set, the slips engage the casing and prevent axial and rotational movement of the whipstock during milling. If required, the anchor can be fitted with packing elements to seal the annulus and isolate the wellbore below the whipstock. The anchor is supplied with a spacer sub that separates it from the whipstock, reducing the effect of the lateral reaction force on the anchor when the whipstock is pressed against the casing wall. The anchor is retrievable, so the whipstock assembly can be recovered after the sidetracking operation is complete.

OPEN-HOLE MECHANICAL ANCHOR

The open-hole mechanical anchor sets the whipstock in an uncased wellbore with hole sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 12 1/4 in. It is run and set with the whipstock setting tool for open hole. It fixes the whipstock mechanically by engaging the open-hole formation and holds the required whipstock position during cementing. It is intended for open-hole applications where a conventional casing anchor cannot be used.
