Whipstock hydraulic packer

A hydraulically actuated and retrievable packer designed for integration with the whipstock system to provide reliable wellbore isolation during sidetracking operations. The packer is hydraulically activated through the running string and provides a pressure-tight seal between the whipstock assembly and the wellbore.

About the tool

The packer provides effective pressure isolation during the sidetracking operation and maintains sealing integrity under the specified well conditions. Its retrievable design allows the packer to be released and recovered together with the whipstock assembly when required.

The packer can be supplied as an optional component of the hydraulic anchoring configuration, providing additional wellbore isolation where required by the selected sidetracking application.

Design advantages

  • For casing sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 13 3/8 in., with various casing wall thicknesses.
  • Hydraulic activation through the running string
  • Reliable wellbore isolation during sidetracking operations
  • Pressure-tight sealing between the whipstock assembly and wellbore
  • Retrievable configuration for recovery after completion of the operation
  • Optional integration with the hydraulic anchoring system
