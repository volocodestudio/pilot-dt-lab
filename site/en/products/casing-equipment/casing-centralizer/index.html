Casing centralizer

Casing centralizers are designed to centralize casing strings in the wellbore during running and cementing operations.

Category
Casing equipment
Request a selection

About the tool

The use of centralizers helps maintain a uniform annular clearance between the casing and the wellbore wall, reducing the risk of casing-to-formation contact and promoting more effective and continuous cement coverage around the casing.

Proper casing centralization improves cement placement and contributes to reliable zonal isolation. It also helps reduce the risk of corrosion by ensuring a more uniform cement sheath around the casing.

The centerpieces are made to fit any size of casing
