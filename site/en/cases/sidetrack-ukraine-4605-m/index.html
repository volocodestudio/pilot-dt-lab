Sidetrack operation with the PL-WSMSA “ANTHEUS” whipstock system

UkrgasvydobuvannyaUkraineOnshore

Being verified

Objectives

  • Restoration of an existing well by milling a casing window and sidetracking in a single run to establish a new borehole, maintaining directional control and reducing NPT.
  • Mill a casing window and establish a new wellbore in a single trip.
  • Maintain effective directional control throughout sidetracking operations.
  • Minimize nonproductive time (NPT) and optimize overall operational efficiency.

Results

  • The PL-WSMSA whipstock system and milling BHA were successfully deployed to the planned depth.
  • Correct orientation was achieved using a gyro tool.
  • The mechanical anchor was set, and the milling BHA was released as planned.
  • Milling operations were completed, achieving a full casing exit and successfully drilling a 26 ft (8 m) rathole to initiate the sidetrack.

Value to client

  • A full-gauge casing window was successfully achieved in a single run, significantly reducing tripping time and associated operational costs.
  • A reliable casing exit enabled efficient sidetrack drilling with a positive displacement motor (PDM), ensuring operational continuity.
