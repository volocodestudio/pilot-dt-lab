PL-WSHSA

NEXUS™ whipstock system with hydraulic anchor

The PL-WSHSA whipstock system with hydraulic anchor is designed for efficient and reliable casing window milling and sidetracking operations. The anchor is set hydraulically: pressure is applied through the drill string and passes through the milling assembly and the external hydraulic line to the anchor, where multiple slips engage the casing and lock the anchor securely in place. The whipstock can be oriented with an MWD system before setting, and no cement plug or bridge plug is needed as a base. APPLICATIONS

The PL-WSHSA whipstock system with hydraulic anchor is designed for efficient and reliable casing window milling and sidetracking operations. The anchor is set hydraulically: pressure is applied through the drill string and passes through the milling assembly and the external hydraulic line to the anchor, where multiple slips engage the casing and lock the anchor securely in place. The whipstock can be oriented with an MWD system before setting, and no cement plug or bridge plug is needed as a base. Reliable fixation and accurate orientation allow the window to be milled in a single run, which reduces rig time and non-productive time (NPT) and lowers overall operating costs. APPLICATIONS:

  • Milling a window in casing strings
  • Deflecting the drill string in a predetermined direction
  • Controlled kickoff of a new wellbore
  • Initiating lateral sidetracks
  • Bypassing damaged or problematic intervals, fish or lost-in-hole equipment

Design advantages

  • Reliable fixation through hydraulic activation
  • No set-down weight required for anchor setting
  • Controlled activation at a predetermined hydraulic pressure
  • Circulation maintained below the setting pressure
  • MWD-based whipstock orientation before setting
  • No cement plug or bridge plug required
  • Window milled in a single run
  • Multiple slips reduce the risk of whipstock rotation or displacement during milling
  • Suitable for vertical and deviated wells
  • Suitable for casing sizes from 5 1/2 in. to 13 3/8 in. with various wall thicknesses
  • Retrievable design for recovery of the whipstock assembly

The system includes*

  • Whipstock concave
  • Anchor
  • Milling system
  • Hydraulic kit
