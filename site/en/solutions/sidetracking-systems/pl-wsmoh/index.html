PL-WSMOH

ATLAS™ whipstock system open hole

The PL-WSMOH open-hole whipstock system is designed for sidetracking and lateral drilling in open-hole wellbores. The whipstock is anchored in the open-hole section by a mechanical slip system that engages the formation, and is then cemented in place to prevent rotation or displacement during drilling. The system can be installed in open-hole sections from 5 1/2 in. to 12 1/4 in.

Category
Sidetracking

APPLICATIONS

  • Initiating a new lateral wellbore from the main wellbore
  • Establishing controlled directional drilling trajectories
  • Bypassing obstructions or problematic wellbore intervals
  • Correcting or bypassing a deviated or crooked wellbore section
  • Improving reservoir access through multilateral drilling

Design advantages

  • Designed for uncased wellbores where a conventional casing anchor cannot be used
  • Suitable for open-hole sections from 5 1/2 in. to 12 1/4 in.
  • Whipstock concave available with 2°, 2.5° and 3° inclination angles
  • Mechanical slip anchoring followed by cementing for reliable whipstock fixation during drilling
  • Drilling fluid circulation maintained during operations, supporting wellbore stability and safe setting
  • Accurate azimuthal orientation using MWD or gyro systems
  • Controlled and secure release of the setting tool from the whipstock after setting

The system includes

  • Whipstock concave
  • Anchor
  • Milling system
  • Setting tool for whipstock open hole
